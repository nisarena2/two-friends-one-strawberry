# Flying High at Sunset

A warm photograph of a father lifting his little daughter high into the
air at golden-hour sunset in a park, both of them laughing — her arms
spread wide like wings, his proud smile looking up at her. The image is
1024x1024 and under 1 MiB. Pure joy, no staging — just a dad, his
little girl, and the last light of the day.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
